City grants cooling water permit

Published: 13 November 2026 · Permit granted

The permit covers groundwater and three streams: Malvasierbach, Stadtbach and Proviantbach. The 13 November gazette (page 302) gives the inspection dates: 20 November to 3 December. Read the decision and plans online or at the environment office, Schießgrabenstraße 4.

How do we choose stories?

We select a few local developments and put the most recent notice first. Each date tells you when the source published its update. A meeting or decision can have a different date.

What counts as a decision?

An agenda tells you what a committee plans to discuss. It does not tell you what the committee decided. We report an individual vote only when the minutes or a roll call name the voter.

Which representatives appear here?

We include directly elected officials and a selection of members elected from party lists. Each profile explains the person’s connection to the city. The linked directories cover more members.

How current is this?

This is a dated snapshot. It changes only when the records are checked again. Follow the source links for later developments.
