Council adds £2,500 to Christmas lights funding

The district council approved an extra £2,500 contribution towards Stratford-upon-Avon’s 2026 Christmas lights.

Stratford-upon-Avon town · Snapshot 15 November 2026

Responsible: Stratford-on-Avon District Council · Leader’s delegated decisionRead the official update ↗

What this means for you

The contribution helps close a £10,000 funding shortfall for the town display; partners cover the remaining £7,500.

This is an additional council contribution, alongside funding from the town council, Town Trust, BID, businesses and sponsors.

Check the funding breakdown

The official record explains the shortfall, partner contributions and alternative considered.

Read the funding breakdown ↗

Where things stand

The decision records higher energy costs and standing charges. Reducing or cancelling the display was considered as an alternative.

The funding decision is not an installation record or an events timetable.
