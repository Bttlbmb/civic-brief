City · Mayor and city council

Dr. Florian Freund · SPD

Augsburg voters elected Florian Freund as mayor in 2026. He runs the city administration and chairs the council, which has 60 other elected members. Augsburg is an independent city. It runs its own driving licence office. In Bobingen, that is a district responsibility.

The council chose two of its members as deputies. Dirk Wurm (SPD) is second mayor and Bernd Zitzelsberger (CSU) is third mayor.

Interview · Published in October 2026

Young reporters from Logi-Fox asked Florian Freund about his work as mayor. The children suggested more playgrounds and more school holiday activities for teenagers.

Swabia · Regional assembly

Erwin Gerblinger and Martin Sailer · both CSU

Augsburg belongs to the region of Swabia. Voters in Augsburg-Stadt-Ost (701) elected Erwin Gerblinger to its assembly. Those in Augsburg-Stadt-West (702) elected Martin Sailer. The region provides services including support for people with disabilities.

Sailer also heads Augsburg district. His role here comes from a separate election to the regional assembly.

Bavaria · Bavarian state parliament

Andreas Jäckel and Leo Dietz · both CSU

Your neighbourhood determines which constituency you live in. Augsburg-Stadt-Ost (701) elected Andreas Jäckel. Augsburg-Stadt-West (702) elected Leo Dietz. Both vote on laws for all of Bavaria.

Voters also elected members through party lists covering the whole of Swabia. The state parliament’s map shows who they are.

Germany · German Bundestag

Claudia Roth (Greens) and Raimond Scheirich (AfD)

Augsburg-Stadt (251) had no directly elected MP in 2025. Volker Ullrich (CSU) got the most local candidate votes, but the CSU had too few party votes in Bavaria for that seat. Claudia Roth and Raimond Scheirich also ran here. Each entered the Bundestag through their party’s Bavarian list.

Vote on childcare · 12 November 2026

The Bundestag passed a bill to improve early learning in childcare centres. The voting record shows how each member voted. It gives no reasons for their votes.

EU · European Parliament

Germany elects 96 MEPs through party lists. In Bavaria, residents choose a Bavarian or nationwide party list. The profile below features one MEP with local ties.

A locally connected MEP

Markus Ferber (CSU)

Augsburg has no separate seat in European elections. Residents vote for party lists. Markus Ferber was born in Augsburg and won a seat on the CSU’s Bavarian list in 2024.

Ferber’s party is the CSU. In the European Parliament, he sits with the European People’s Party group (EPP).

Negotiating position 12 November 2026

Parliament agrees changes to EU disaster-response rules

The European Parliament agreed changes to proposed rules for disaster and health emergency support. It sent the file back for talks with the Council, where national governments are represented. Final EU rules still need agreement.

Augsburg firefighters responded to 356 calls during the first days of the June 2024 floods. This EU vote does not assign money to projects in Augsburg.
