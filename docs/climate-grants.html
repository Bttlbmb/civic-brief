£30,000 approved for local flooding and solar projects

The district council authorised three £10,000 awards: flood-resilience work in Alcester and solar projects at two community venues.

Alcester · Clifford Chambers · Snitterfield · Snapshot 15 November 2026

Responsible: Stratford-on-Avon District Council · delegated funding decisionRead the official update ↗

What this means for you

The approved work targets Alcester’s gullies and culverts, Clifford Chambers Social Club and Snitterfield Village Hall.

The £30,000 covers these three selected awards, not the whole district funding round.

Read the allocations and printed notice. Project completion dates are not established by these records.

Where things stand

Alcester Town Council’s award is for surveying and clearing gullies and culverts. Clifford Chambers’ award covers solar panels and a battery; Snitterfield’s covers a solar installation.

Approval does not establish that the money has been paid or the work completed.

Source dates: The online record dates the decision 10 November. The printed notice records political approval on 24 October, monitoring approval on 10 November and effectiveness on 11 November.
