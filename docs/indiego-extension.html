Council renews support for the on-demand bus service

The district council approved a further 12 months for its existing IndieGo Plus agreement with Warwickshire County Council, subject to continued performance.

Stratford-on-Avon district service · Snapshot 15 November 2026

Responsible: Stratford-on-Avon District Council · Head of Housing, Revenues and Customer ServicesRead the official update ↗

What this means for you

This supports the existing booked bus service. It does not promise a new route or guarantee a journey.

The decision extends the current agreement. It sets no new fares or routes.

Using IndieGo Plus

Check the county’s current operating area and booking guidance before planning a trip.

Where things stand

The district contributes £120,000 a year to the county-commissioned service. This is existing support, not £120,000 of newly announced extra funding.

The record does not give the extension’s start and end dates. Bookings remain subject to availability.
