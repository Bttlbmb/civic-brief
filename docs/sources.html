Snapshot 15 November 2026

Checked on 15 November 2026. This is a manually curated snapshot for Stratford-on-Avon parliamentary constituency. It is an independent prototype, not a council or Parliament service.

How we choose the brief

We select a small number of official updates with a practical consequence, a public opportunity to respond, or a decision still to come. Selection is editorial and does not cover everything happening locally.

Each story separates what happened, what it means for residents, the remaining decision stages and any documented next step. A consultation is not an adopted policy; a Cabinet recommendation is not final council approval.

Place and boundaries

Stratford-on-Avon is the parliamentary constituency. Stratford-upon-Avon is the town. The similarly named district council area is larger than the constituency and is covered by two MPs.

News from district or county programmes is included when it is relevant to the constituency. A countywide budget is never presented as a constituency allocation. Councillors represent wards or divisions, which need an address check. We link to the official finders rather than guessing your councillor.

Town and parish responsibilities vary. The directory is linked on the representatives page; a complete parish-by-parish service catalogue is outside this MVP.

Current county and district councils remain the service contacts in this snapshot. Proposed reorganisation concerns future councils; it has not replaced the current tiers.

Where sources need interpretation

Climate grants: the online record dates the decision 10 November. Its printed notice separates political approval on 24 October, monitoring approval on 10 November and effectiveness on 11 November. The story uses the 11 November publication date and explains these stages. The £30,000 is a selected subset of awards, not the whole fund.

Local Plan: the dated 11 November joint release expects submission in early 2027. The plan homepage still refers to a December deadline. We use the dated release and show the discrepancy on the story page.

Bus programme: the county announcement describes Cabinet approval but also says Full Council sign-off is still needed. We label the programme as awaiting final approval.

MP activity: sources & selection

The MP activity overview lists the latest ten recorded divisions shown on her official member page on 15 November 2026, covering 3–4 November. Four policy examples are explained in more detail. The set includes seven Budget resolutions, with five No votes and two Ayes. This is not attendance, a complete policy history, or a score.

Three spoken contributions and one bill presentation are selected from official Hansard. Their labels distinguish a question, an intervention, a speech and first reading. A request or proposal is not an achieved policy outcome. Each item links its transcript.

The media section links two original BBC reports verified in a browser. The September item explicitly reports a BBC CWR interview; the October item carries MP comments. Neither report establishes an exact broadcast date or an available replay. The selection is not a complete media diary.

What this MVP does and does not cover

The brief includes three recent decisions and three consultations or upcoming decisions, a constituency MP profile, an activity overview with ten recorded votes, speeches and media examples, an election result and practical contact routes. It does not provide an exhaustive news feed, a live address lookup, a complete voting analysis or a complete councillor directory.

There is no account, subscription, analytics or form submission in this prototype. Contact and reporting links take you to the relevant official service. This brief does not collect your address or send a message on your behalf.

Checking and updates

If a source changes, use the official contact links to confirm the current position. For a correction to this prototype, tell its creator which story or source needs review.
