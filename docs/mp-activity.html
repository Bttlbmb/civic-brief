Speeches, parliamentary work and media items are selected examples, not a complete activity log. A missing vote does not establish abstention or absence. These records do not measure workload, effectiveness or party loyalty.

Parliament supplies the named votes and transcripts; the BBC supplies the media reports. Article publication dates are separate from broadcast dates. Tax resolutions can take provisional legal effect; a first reading is an early bill stage.
