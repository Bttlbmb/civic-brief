Development plan heads for December decisions

The two district councils are processing consultation responses before December meetings on submitting the South Warwickshire Local Plan.

Stratford-on-Avon & Warwick districts · Snapshot 15 November 2026

Responsible: Stratford-on-Avon & Warwick District CouncilsRead the official update ↗

What this means for you

The plan will guide future development. The consultation has closed; the plan has not yet been adopted.

A submission vote is one stage in making a local plan. It is not planning permission for an individual development.

Watch for the December papers

Follow the official plan website for the meeting papers and examination updates. The councils expect the joint committee to consider submission on 10 December and full councils on 14 December.

Follow the official plan ↗

Where things stand

The councils say they are collating responses for the independent Planning Inspector. The next council decisions concern submitting the plan for examination.

The consultation is closed. The dated 11 November update expects submission in early 2027; the plan website still refers to a December deadline.
