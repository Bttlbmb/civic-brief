Augsburg asks what should change in the city

The city is collecting ideas for its future until 29 November. Have your say online or on paper at the public information office on Rathausplatz. The city council will decide later.

Approved Published: 3 Nov 2026

Council funds more secondary school places

In October, Augsburg City Council approved up to €2.375 million towards expanding Bischof-Ulrich-Realschule. The expansion would create 75 to 98 extra places for pupils who live in Augsburg.
