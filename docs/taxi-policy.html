Help shape the district’s taxi rules

The district council is consulting on its draft taxi policy for 2027–2032, including accessibility and passenger safety.

Stratford-on-Avon district · Snapshot 15 November 2026

Responsible: Stratford-on-Avon District CouncilRead the official update ↗

What this means for you

If you use taxis or private-hire cars, you can comment by 21 December.

These are proposed licensing rules. The consultation is your opportunity to give feedback before the policy is finalised.

Send your comments by 21 December

Read the draft policy and response instructions on the council’s consultation page. You can email comments to the licensing officer listed there.

Read the draft & respond ↗

Where things stand

The proposals concern how the council licenses taxis and private-hire vehicles, drivers and operators. The consultation includes proposed changes to accessibility and safeguarding requirements.

The draft is not an adopted policy. It does not change taxi rules immediately.
