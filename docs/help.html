Missed bin or recycling collection

Stratford-on-Avon District Council

The district asks you to report a missed collection within two working days. Check its conditions before reporting, including that the bin was put out correctly.

Pothole or road problem

Warwickshire County Council

Report a road problem through the county’s highway service. It has separate routes for potholes, streetlights, obstructions and other highway issues.

Report a highway problem ↗Find your county councillor ↗
Planning application or development

Stratford-on-Avon District Council

Use the district planning service to find an application, read the documents and check whether comments are being accepted. The deadline is specific to each application.

Primary school place

Warwickshire County Council

For September 2027 Reception or Junior entry, apply by 15 January 2027 at 4pm UK time. The county page explains eligibility, house moves and late applications. Other entry routes have different arrangements.

Taxi policy or licensing

Stratford-on-Avon District Council

The district licenses taxis and private-hire vehicles. A consultation on its draft 2027–2032 policy is open until 21 December 2026.

National policy or government casework

Manuela Perteghella · Your MP

For national policy or problems with a government department, contact your constituency MP. Include your name and full address. Her office can raise a case; it cannot guarantee the outcome.
