City · Mayor and city council

Michael Ammer · Freie Bürger-Union

Bobingen voters elected Michael Ammer as mayor. He runs the city administration and chairs the council. Alongside him, 24 elected councillors decide on matters such as city buildings and roads.

The council chose two of its members as deputies. Herwig Leiter (CSU) is second mayor and Dr. Armin Bergmann (SPD) is third mayor.

District · Augsburg district

Martin Sailer · CSU

Bobingen is part of Augsburg district. Its voters help elect the district head and district council. Martin Sailer runs the district administration and chairs the council. One district job is issuing driving licences.

Rainer Naumann (Freie Wähler) is a district councillor from Bobingen. Voters across the whole district elect him and his fellow councillors.

Swabia · Regional assembly

Klaus Förster · CSU

Bobingen is in the Augsburg-Land-Süd constituency (705). Its voters directly elected Klaus Förster to the Swabian regional assembly. The region provides services including support for people with disabilities.

Bavaria · Bavarian state parliament

Carolina Trautner · CSU

Bobingen is in the Augsburg-Land-Süd constituency (705). Its voters directly elected Carolina Trautner to the state parliament, where she votes on laws for all of Bavaria.

Voters also elected members through party lists covering the whole of Swabia. The state parliament’s map shows who they are.

Funding for the village hall · March and April 2026

Carolina Trautner and other members asked for €625,000 for the Waldberg-Kreuzanger village hall. On 23 April, the state parliament approved a budget that included the project. The budget does not say whether the money has been paid.

Germany · German Bundestag

Hansjörg Durz (CSU) and Heike Heubach (SPD)

Bobingen is in the Augsburg-Land constituency (252). Its voters elected Hansjörg Durz to the Bundestag in 2025. Heike Heubach also stood here. She won her seat through the SPD’s party list for Bavaria.

Vote on childcare · 12 November 2026

The Bundestag passed a bill to improve early learning in childcare centres. The vote did not award funding to any specific centre in Bobingen.

EU · European Parliament

Germany elects 96 MEPs through party lists. In Bavaria, residents choose a Bavarian or nationwide party list. The profile below features one MEP with local ties.

A locally connected MEP

Markus Ferber (CSU)

Bobingen has no separate seat in European elections. Residents vote for party lists. Markus Ferber won a seat on the CSU’s Bavarian list in 2024. He served on Bobingen City Council from 1990 to 1999.

Ferber’s party is the CSU. In the European Parliament, he sits with the European People’s Party group (EPP).

Negotiating position 12 November 2026

Parliament agrees changes to EU disaster-response rules

The European Parliament agreed changes to proposed rules for disaster and health emergency support. It sent the file back for talks with the Council, where national governments are represented. Final EU rules still need agreement.

The Holzhausen flood basin helped protect Bobingen in June 2024. This EU vote does not assign money to projects in Bobingen.
