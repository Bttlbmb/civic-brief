Bus upgrade plan awaits final sign-off

Warwickshire’s Cabinet has backed a £12.051 million bus infrastructure programme for 2026–2030. Full Council still needs to approve it.

Warwickshire · countywide programme · Snapshot 15 November 2026

Responsible: Warwickshire County CouncilRead the official update ↗

What this means for you

Alcester, Bidford, Henley, Shipston and Studley could benefit. Specific stops and dates are not confirmed.

The programme concerns bus infrastructure. It does not itself promise extra bus services, lower fares or an upgrade at every stop.

Follow the final funding decision

Read the Cabinet record and follow the council’s update for final approval and individual schemes. No resident response deadline is announced in this update.

Read the Cabinet decision ↗

Where things stand

Cabinet has recommended a countywide programme of bus interchange improvements. The council’s announcement says final sign-off will be sought from Full Council in December.

The £12.051 million is a countywide programme total, not money allocated to this constituency. Final council approval is still pending.
